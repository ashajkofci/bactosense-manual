## 7.1 Maintenance by the user {#section-7-1}

To ensure proper device functioning, the following maintenance can be undertaken by the user.

:::gloves[Wear nitrile gloves]

Avoid contamination of the equipment. Wear nitrile gloves when handling tubings and online sampling devices and any component which comes in contact with them.

:::

:::warning[Before maintenance]

**Before performing any maintenance operation**

- Close the On/Off valves at the water pipes connection.
- Disconnect the mains power supply to avoid any risk of short circuits.

:::

### 7.1.1 Cleaning {#section-7-1-1}

In the event of biological contamination, the BactoSwitch can be cleaned in the following ways:

| AGENT | CONDITIONS |
| --- | --- |
| **Chlorine** | Flowing water with up to 10’000 mg/L of active chlorine is allowed. :::warning[Chlorine cleaning] • Wear appropriate personal protective equipment.<br />• Ensure that all tube junctions are carefully secured and tightened. ::: |
| **Hot water** | Flowing water at temperatures up to 85 °C, with a reduced maximum pressure of 2 bar, and for up to 15 min. :::warning[Hot water cleaning] • The installation must include a pressure-limiting device at the inlet tubings input, to ensure that the pressure cannot exceed 2 bar.<br />• Ensure that all tube junctions are carefully secured and tightened (see [Installation of the fluidic lines](04-installation.md#section-4-4)).<br />• The front cover must be installed when flowing hot water in the system.<br />• Durations longer than 15 min may heat the accessible device surfaces and cause skin burns.<br />• Wear appropriate personal protective equipment. ::: |

Contact support@bnovate.com for questions.

### 7.1.2 Replacement of input tubings {#section-7-1-2}

In the event of persistent biological or non-biological (e.g. iron, manganese) contamination of the tubings, these can be swapped by the user.

:::warning[Before replacing tubings]

**Before disconnecting the tubings**

- Close the On/Off valves at the water pipes connection.
- Disconnect the mains power supply to avoid any risk of short circuits.

:::

## 7.2 Preventive maintenance by service professional {#section-7-2}

A yearly preventive maintenance intervention must be scheduled to replace wear parts, update software and firmware, and verify proper functioning of the device. This is important to prevent failures, optimize the performance and reliability, and ensure a long product lifetime.

The preventive maintenance must only be conducted by specifically trained service professionals.

Your local service partner can provide you with information about the preventive maintenance schedule and the available options.
