## 9.1 Contacting customer service {#section-9-1}

If you have any questions, please contact the bNovate support team under support@bnovate.com, or the service centre responsible in your country or region.

A list of all bNovate representatives is available online at [www.bnovate.com/distribution-partners](https://www.bnovate.com/distribution-partners).
