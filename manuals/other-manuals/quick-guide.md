This Quick Guide helps you get started with BactoSense, your solution for the automated measurement of the concentration of microorganisms in water.

## Before getting started {#before-getting-started}

:::warning[Follow the user manual instructions]

Read the [BactoSense user manual](/bactosense/) before installing or operating the instrument. Pay particular attention to the [safety chapter](/bactosense/safety) and the safety information throughout the manual. Make sure you always have access to the user manual. Find the latest instrument and accessory manuals at [www.bnovate.com/docs](https://www.bnovate.com/docs).

:::

### Operating conditions

| Parameter | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Temperature | 5–30 °C (41–86 °F) |
| Humidity | 10–90 % RH |
| Location | Indoor use, maximum 2000 m (6600 ft) above sea level |

### Sample conditions

| Parameter | Range |
| --- | --- |
| Auto mode, at the online sampling device inlet | Flow rate 200–400 mL/min (0.05–0.10 gal/min); pressure maximum 0.5 bar (7.3 psi) |
| Temperature | 5–40 °C (41–104 °F) |
| Turbidity | 0–10 FTU, 0–10 FNU |
| Conductivity | 0–100,000 μS/cm at 20 °C |
| Chlorine | Maximum 3 mg/L |
| Iron | Maximum 200 μg/L |
| Manganese | Maximum 50 μg/L |
| pH | 5–12 |

## Measuring in auto mode {#auto-mode}

To measure automatically and at predefined intervals from a connected water source:

1. Make sure the correct online sampling device is installed and the sample conditions are met.
2. In the **Home** menu, press **Auto mode** and **Start**.
3. On the time series graph view, press **Start**.
4. Select **Online Sampling Device (with Backflush)** and configure the measurement series.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to start measuring.

## Measuring in manual mode {#manual-mode}

To measure individual grab samples collected in sample tubes:

1. Make sure the manual sampling device is installed correctly.
2. In the **Home** menu, press **Manual mode** and **Start**.
3. Select **Water analysis** and configure the measurement.
4. Attach a 5 mL sample tube containing a 2–5 mL water sample to the sampling device.
5. Follow the on-screen instructions to start the measurement.

## Reviewing results {#results}

In the **Home** menu, press **Results (Manual)** or **Results (Auto)** and select the desired measurement.

## Handling cartridges {#cartridges}

The cartridge contains all the reagents needed for measurements and self-cleaning.

- Icons in the top bar indicate the filling level of a cartridge.
- Find the expiration date and storage conditions on the cartridge or under **System Info**.
- Arrange a cartridge refill once the level is low or the cartridge is about to expire.
- To install, remove or exchange a cartridge, press **Maintenance → Cartridge change** and follow the on-screen instructions.

## Relocating or storing your BactoSense {#storage}

Careful decommissioning, transport, storage and recommissioning are essential. Consult [Storage, transport and disposal](/bactosense/storage-transport-and-disposal).

## Qualification {#qualification}

BactoSense is calibration free.

For operating environments requiring system suitability testing, use the System Suitability Test Kit (SST Kit).

For other environments, an instrument qualification using the [Validation Kit](validation-kit.md) is recommended:

- After shipping or other long-distance transport exposing the instrument to vibration or shock.
- After storing the instrument for more than one month.
- If there are indications that measurement results could be erroneous.

## Keeping your BactoSense clean {#cleaning}

The Wash Station keeps the manual sampling device clean. Use it:

- Before switching BactoSense off.
- After measuring a highly concentrated sample.
- If the instrument will be idle for more than six hours.
- If the manual sampling device has been newly installed.

The [Cleaning Kit](cleaning-kit.md) clears biological and non-biological contaminations. Use it:

- When facing persistent contamination.
- If the needle of the manual sampling device has visible deposits.
- After stopping in an error state with a loaded sample.
- If **High baseline** or **Air bubble detection** warnings frequently appear.
