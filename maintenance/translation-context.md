# Context for automated manual translations

These are technical product manuals from bNovate Technologies. Write for trained instrument operators, laboratory staff and service personnel. Use clear, concise instructions that help readers identify the actual controls and carry out the English procedure accurately.

## Products and scope

- BactoSense is a rapid bacterial monitoring instrument. Its manual covers installation, operation, measurements, results, configuration, maintenance and troubleshooting.
- BactoSwitch is the multi-line selector accessory used with BactoSense. Keep sample-line selection and accessory operation distinct from the BactoSense measurement and analysis functions.
- BactoCloud is a separate service. Preserve distinctions between the native instrument interface, its web interface and BactoCloud; do not substitute one interface's navigation or features for another's.
- Use the supplied manual metadata and introduction to establish the product, software version and document scope. Chapter and section identifiers connect procedures across the manual and its translations.

## Translation priorities

1. The current English chapter determines meaning, procedures, specifications and safety requirements. Never import superseded instructions from an older translation or add information from general knowledge.
2. `maintenance/translation-glossary.md` determines preferred terminology and exceptions, including scientific acronyms, literal controls, quantities and the instructional register for each language.
3. The current translation, translated introduction, chapter outline and neighboring translated excerpts provide terminology and style context. Use them where consistent with current English and the glossary. They may contain instructions that the English source has since changed.
4. Preserve all qualifications and the force of safety language. Keep quantities such as counts, concentrations and percentages distinct. Do not translate scientific gates as physical doors or valves.
5. Keep the voice and terminology consistent across headings, procedures, image descriptions, notices, tables and index entries. Translate the complete document, including its descriptive frontmatter.

This file is context for the translation agent; do not insert it into the published manual. Structural validation is separate from review of linguistic and scientific accuracy.
