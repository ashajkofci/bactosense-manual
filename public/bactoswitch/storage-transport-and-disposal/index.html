8. Storage, Transport and Disposal

8.1 Storage​

Wear nitrile gloves

Avoid contamination of the equipment. Wear nitrile gloves when handling tubings and online sampling devices and any component which comes in contact with them.

Preparations

WORK STEPADDITIONAL INFO / IMAGES
1.Terminate any ongoing measurement series.
2.Close the On/Off valves at the water pipes connection to stop the water flow through the BactoSwitch tubings and online sampling device.
3.Disconnect the BactoSwitch mains power supply to avoid any risk of short circuits.
4.Remove the power connector plug from the power connector socket, and reinstall the power connector protection cap.
5.Remove the USB-C cable from both the BactoSwitch and the BactoSense, and reinstall both connector protection caps.
6.Remove the input line tubings from the BactoSwitch by (1) pressing on the quick connector ring and (2) pulling on the tubing.
7.Disconnect the input line tubings from the water pipes, and drain the tubings.
8.Detach the online sampling device from the BactoSense by loosening its knurled ring and pulling it downwards.
9.Detach the protective cap from the BactoSense manual sampling device (turn the knurled ring, not the cap) and install it on the online sampling device.

Install the manual sampling device on the BactoSense.
10.Detach the online sampling device from the BactoSwitch by pressing on the quick connector rings and pulling on the tubings. Drain the sampling device tubings.
11.Detach the outlet (drain) tubing by pressing on the quick connector ring and pulling on the tubing, and drain the tubing.
Do not use compressed air

Flow meter damage by pressurized air

Do not connect pressurized air to the BactoSwitch input connectors in an attempt to drain it: this may irreversibly damage the flow meter.

Storage

Store the BactoSwitch and the accessories listed in Table 4-1 in their original packaging and in the following storage conditions:

  • Temperature between 10 °C and 30 °C (50 °F – 86 °F)
  • Relative humidity between 10 % and 70 %
  • Non-vibrating, non-corrosive, dark and clean environment
  • Store the tubings and online sampling device in a clean, dry plastic bag

Re-installation

After storage, follow the general installation instructions described in Installation.

8.2 Transport​

Follow the shipping instructions

Adhere to shipping instructions

bNovate does not take responsibility for deliveries which do not comply with our shipping regulations and will charge for repairs. Please contact support@bnovate.com if the original packaging is no longer available or you need help with packaging.

  • Pack the BactoSwitch in its original packaging with foam inserts.
  • Ship on a pallet or skid.

8.3 Returns​

In case you return the BactoSwitch to bNovate or a distribution partner, please follow the instructions in this section.

Decontaminate before returning

Do not return contaminated materials

Instruments that have come into contact with hazardous media must not be sent without prior decontamination and accompanied certificate of decontamination. Contact support@bnovate.com for more information.

Preparations

WORK STEP
1.If the instrument has been exposed to hazardous substances, fully decontaminate it and provide a certificate of decontamination.
2.Follow the preparation procedure given in Storage.
3.Pack the BactoSwitch and its accessories listed in Table 4-1 in its original packaging with foam inserts. Tubings and the online sampling device must have been drained and packed in a clean plastic bag.

Return

Ship the BactoSwitch to bNovate or the distribution partner, adhering to the transportation recommendations given in Transport and the general storage temperature and humidity requirements listed in Storage.

8.4 Recycling and disposal​

The disposal of the BactoSwitch has to be carried out in compliance with regional statutory regulations. The BactoSwitch contains no environmentally damaging sources of radiation.
